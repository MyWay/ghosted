# Privacy policy: Ghosted

_Last updated: 2026-09-29_

Ghosted runs entirely inside your browser.

**What it reads.** On x.com only, it reads the responses the X website itself loads when you open
your own followers / following lists and your profile (account ids, handles, display names,
avatar URLs, follower counts), and notes when you click Unfollow on x.com. It reads the `twid`
cookie only to learn your numeric account id. It never reads passwords, messages, or auth tokens.

**Where it goes.** Everything is stored locally in the extension's own storage on your device.
There is no server, no analytics, no tracking, and nothing is sold or shared.

**The only outbound traffic** is optional and configured by you: if you enable Telegram or Discord
notifications, a short summary (e.g. "3 unfollowed you: @a, @b, @c") is sent to the chat or
webhook you entered.

**Your control.** Export or delete all data at any time from Dashboard → Settings. Uninstalling
the extension deletes its storage.
